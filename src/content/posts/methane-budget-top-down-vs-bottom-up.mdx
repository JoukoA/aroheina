# A

**Abatement Cost Curve (MACC):** A graphical tool that ranks greenhouse gas reduction options by net cost per tonne of gas abated ($/t CH4), showing negative-cost (self-funding) and positive-cost measures.

**Activity Factor:** A statistical multiplier that relates a level of operational activity (for example hours of compressor operation or throughput volume) to the rate of emissions produced.

**Anemometer:** An instrument that measures ambient wind speed during OGI or high-volume sampling, to check compliance with maximum wind-speed limits such as those in EPA Appendix K.

**API 622 / API 624 / API 641:** American Petroleum Institute standards for type testing fugitive-emission performance of valve packing (622), rising-stem valves with graphite packing (624) and quarter-turn valves (641).

**Approved Vendor List (AVL):** A corporate procurement register of manufacturers whose components (valves, actuators, seals) meet certified low-emission standards such as ISO 15848-1.

**Associated Gas:** Natural gas found with crude oil in a reservoir, either dissolved in the oil or as a gas cap.

# B

**Bagging (High-Volume Sampling):** A Level 4 technique in which a flexible impermeable bag or shroud is sealed over a leaking component to capture and measure the total leak rate (kg/h).

**Basin-Wide Screening:** Broad aerial or satellite surveys that identify major regional emission sources and super-emitter events across multi-operator fields.

**Blowdown:** The intentional depressurisation of process equipment, compressor casings or pipeline segments for maintenance, emergency isolation or turnarounds.

**Bottom-Up Inventory:** An emissions estimate built by identifying individual components or sources, measuring or estimating each one’s emission rate and summing them to a facility total.

# C

**Casinghead Gas:** Gas that comes from the annulus between the tubing and casing of an oil well; if it is not captured it is vented or flared.

**CIRIS (Certificate Inter-Regional Import System):** MiQ’s set of rules for transferring methane performance data and certificates from a producing region to an importing region, conditioned on proof of physical transport.

**Continuous Emissions Monitoring Systems (CEMS):** Fixed, automated sensor arrays (open-path optical, point electrochemical or metal-oxide) around a facility perimeter that provide continuous detection and measurement.

**Computerised Maintenance Management System (CMMS):** Enterprise software (for example SAP PM or IBM Maximo) used to manage asset maintenance, generate work orders and track failure histories.

**Closed-Loop System:** A design in which gas vents (for example compressor rod packing or relief valve outputs) are captured and routed back to low-pressure suction or sales lines instead of the atmosphere.

# D

**Data Quality Score (PCAF):** A 1-to-5 rating of how an emissions figure was obtained. Score 1 is verified reported emissions, 2 unverified reported emissions, 3 physical-activity-based estimates and 4–5 economic-activity or other estimates. It measures disclosure quality and is not itself a price.

**Destruction and Removal Efficiency (DRE):** The percentage of incoming methane that is destroyed in a flare or thermal oxidiser. On a methane-mass basis: (Win – Wout) / Win

**Detection Limit:** The smallest emission rate a method can reliably detect under stated conditions. It varies with platform, wind, surface and altitude.

**Dump Valve:** An automatic liquid-level control valve on a separation vessel that periodically dumps liquids to storage; frequently a large source when stuck open.

# E

**Emission Factor (EF):** A ratio relating the mass of a pollutant released to an activity unit (for example kg CH4 per hour per valve).

**Enterprise Value Including Cash (EVIC):** The sum of market capitalisation, debt and other claims, used as the denominator in PCAF attribution for listed companies.

**Environmental Attribute Certificate (EAC):** A tradable certificate representing an environmental attribute of an energy product, such as low methane intensity, which can be sold with or separately from the physical product. Renewable-energy certificates and MiQ certificates are examples.

**EU Methane Regulation (EU 2024/1787):** The EU regulation establishing mandatory MRV, LDAR, venting and flaring restrictions and extraterritorial import requirements for oil, gas and coal.

**Extraterritorial Scope:** Legal reach beyond national borders, enforcing foreign supplier compliance through import market access.

# F

**Factory Acceptance Test (FAT):** Testing of equipment at the vendor’s plant against the purchase specification before shipment.

**Fat-Tail Distribution:** A distribution in which a small share of sources accounts for a large share of total emissions. The exact shares vary by dataset.

**Financed Emissions:** Scope 3 Category 15 indirect emissions linked to a financial institution’s lending, underwriting and investments.

**Flame Ionsation Detector (FID):** A handheld sniffer standard under EPA Method 21 that ionises organic vapours in a hydrogen flame to measure hydrocarbon concentration in ppm.

**Flaring:** Controlled combustion of natural gas in an open flare stack or enclosed burner, converting CH4 into CO2 and H2O.

**Fugitive Emissions:** Unintentional gas leaks from mechanical seals, valve packing, flange gaskets, threaded fittings or hatch seals.

# G

**Global Warming Potential (GWP):** The cumulative radiative forcing of a pulse emission of a gas over a time horizon (for example 20 or 100 years), relative to that of the same mass of CO2.

**Gold Standard (OGMP 2.0):** UNEP/IMEO recognition of operators with a credible path (Gold Standard Pathway) to, or completed (Gold Standard Reporting), Level 4 and Level 5 reporting for all material assets within 3 years for operated and 5 years for non-operated ventures. It is assessed annually. 

# H

**High-Volume Sampler:** A portable suction instrument that draws surrounding air at roughly 10–17 m3/h to isolate a leaking component and calculate its mass leak rate.

**Hydroxyl Radical (OH):** The main chemical oxidant in the troposphere that breaks down methane, setting its lifetime at about 12 years.

# I

**Implementation Plan (OGMP):** The commitment document a new OGMP 2.0 member submits, setting out the multi-year roadmap for moving operated and non-operated assets to Level 4/5 reporting.

**Instrument Air System:** A system that uses compressed air, not natural gas, to actuate pneumatic control valves, eliminating routine methane venting.

**International Methane Emissions Observatory (IMEO):** A UNEP body that aggregates, reconciles and publishes global methane data from OGMP 2.0 reports, satellites and studies.

**ISO 15848-1:** The international standard for type testing fugitive emissions from industrial valves. It defines tightness classes (A, B, C, tested with helium or methane) and endurance classes (CO1–CO3 for on-off valves, CC1–CC3 for control valves).

# L

**Leak Detection and Repair (LDAR):** A governed framework combining structured inspections (OGI or Method 21) with mandatory repair protocols, close-out resurveys and digital recordkeeping.

**Level 1–5 (OGMP Tiers):** The five OGMP 2.0 reporting tiers, from Level 1 (venture totals) to Level 4 (source-level measurement) and Level 5 (site-level reconciliation).

**LiDAR (Light Detection and Ranging):** An active optical technique using laser pulses to measure path-integrated methane concentration from airborne or drone platforms.

# M

**Marginal Well:** A low-producing oil or gas well, also called a stripper well. The EPA has noted that marginal wells supply a small share of US output but account for a disproportionate share of methane emissions from the sector, and has said it will propose separate standards for them.

**Mass Flux Rate:** The mass of methane passing through a defined boundary or plume per unit time, in kg/h or t/yr.

**Materiality Threshold:** A rule (for example the OGMP 95% portfolio rule) that decides which assets or source categories must be directly measured at Level 4/5.

**Method 21 (US EPA):** A prescriptive protocol for using handheld concentration sniffers (FID/PID) to detect fugitive leaks by traversing component interfaces.

**Methane Intensity:** Methane emitted divided by methane (or gas or energy) delivered, often in percent. Definitions differ between schemes, so check the denominator.

**Microscopic vs. Telescopic View:** The contrast between source-level bottom-up inventories (microscope, Level 4) and site-level atmospheric measurements (telescope, Level 5).

**MiQ:** An independent not-for-profit, established by RMI and SYSTEMIQ, that certifies oil and gas production facilities on methane intensity, monitoring technology deployment and company practices. Facilities receive a grade from A to F, and certificates are issued per MMBtu in the MiQ Registry.

**Monitoring, Reporting and Verification (MRV):** The system of measuring, documenting and independently verifying emissions data.

# N

**Non-Operated Joint Venture (NOJV):** A venture in which a company holds an equity interest (above 5% for OGMP purposes) but does not control day-to-day operations.

**NSPS OOOOb / EG OOOOc:** US EPA rules under Clean Air Act Section 111 covering zero-emission pneumatic controllers, Appendix K OGI standards and the Super-Emitter Program for new and existing sources.

# O

**Oil & Gas Methane Partnership 2.0 (OGMP 2.0):** The UN-led reporting framework with multi-tier measurement, reconciliation and audit protocols for oil and gas methane.

**Optical Gas Imaging (OGI):** Thermal infrared cameras filtered to a narrow absorption band (3.2–3.4 μm) that make hydrocarbon plumes visible in real time.

# P

**Partnership for Carbon Accounting Financials (PCAF):** An industry-led standard that lets financial institutions measure and disclose financed emissions in lending and investment portfolios.

**Pneumatic Controller:** A field device powered by process natural gas that periodically or continuously vents methane to actuate dump valves, pressure regulators or temperature switches.

# Q

**Quantitative Optical Gas Imaging (QOGI):** OGI with software that converts plume pixel density, distance and thermal contrast (ΔT) into a real-time mass leak rate (kg/h).

# R

**Radiative Forcing:** The net change in the Earth’s energy balance from a change in greenhouse gas concentration, in W/m2.

**Reconciliation:** The workflow of comparing bottom-up inventories (Level 4) with top-down mass flux (Level 5) to identify and resolve differences.

**Reconciliation Ratio (R):** The top-down site mass flux divided by the bottom-up source sum, Qtd/Qbu, reported with its uncertainty. Chapter 4 and OGMP Module C use the same definition.

**Rod Packing:** Flexible sealing rings in a compressor stuffing box that seal gas along the moving piston rod of a reciprocating compressor; a primary source of compressor leaks.

# S

**SCADA:** Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition: an industrial control system that monitors pressure, flow, temperature and valve positions in real time.

**Super-Emitter:** A large release, defined in the US EPA rules as 100 kg/h of methane or more. The term is also used more loosely for any unusually large source. The EPA has announced that it will propose to rescind its Super-Emitter Program.

**Sustainability-Linked Loan (SLL):** Corporate debt whose interest margin rises or falls with performance against verified ESG indicators, such as OGMP Gold Standard attainment.

# T

**Thief Hatch:** An adjustable, spring-loaded access hatch on the roof of an atmospheric storage tank; a major leak source when unlatched or worn.

**Top-Down Measurement:** Quantification of total facility emissions from outside or above the asset boundary (drones, aircraft, satellites, continuous monitors).

**Tunable Laser Absorption Spectroscopy (TLAS):** An optical technique using narrow-linewidth lasers tuned to methane absorption lines to compute path-integrated concentration (ppm·m).

# U

**Uncertainty Propagation:** A mathematical procedure (Monte Carlo simulation or error-propagation formulas) that combines individual measurement errors into a confidence interval (±X%) for reported emissions.

**Unlit Flaring:** A failure mode in which a flare keeps venting raw gas because the pilot is out or the flame has blown out, giving 0% DRE for that gas.

# V

**Vapour Recovery Unit (VRU):** A low-pressure compression package on storage tanks or separators that captures flash gas and routes it to gas pipelines.

**Venting:** The deliberate release of unburned natural gas or hydrocarbon vapours to the atmosphere during operation, depressurisation or maintenance.

# W

**WGS84:** The World Geodetic System 1984 reference frame, used for facility coordinates in OGMP 2.0 templates.

**Work Order:** A maintenance record in a CMMS that specifies location, component tag, leak severity and repair deadline for a detected leak.

# Z

**Zero Routine Flaring and Venting:** A policy commitment to end non-emergency flaring and venting, for example the World Bank initiative and the UK target for 2030.
